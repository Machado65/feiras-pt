# Portugal Fairs · Feiras PT

A calendar of traditional fairs, food and wine festivals, medieval fairs and Christmas markets across Portugal, including Madeira and the Azores. Subscribe once and the fairs show up in your calendar app. The list is checked every month, and events update on their own.

[Português](#português)

## Subscribe

Calendar address (English):

```
https://machado65.github.io/feiras-pt/fairs.ics
```

- **Google Calendar:** [add it with one click](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/render?cid=webcal%3A%2F%2Fmachado65.github.io%2Ffeiras-pt%2Ffairs.ics) on a computer. On Android it then syncs from your Google account; if it doesn't show up, turn on sync for it in the app's settings.
- **iPhone and iPad:** Settings → Apps → Calendar → Calendar Accounts → Add Account → Other → Add Subscribed Calendar, then paste the address.
- **Mac:** Calendar → File → New Calendar Subscription, then paste the address.
- **Outlook:** Add calendar → Subscribe from web, then paste the address.

## What's in it

- Medieval fairs: the Viagem Medieval in Santa Maria da Feira, Óbidos, Silves, Castro Marim...
- Food fairs: smoked meats, cheese, chestnuts, chocolate, convent sweets, seafood...
- Wine festivals: Borba, Alvarinho in Monção, Essência do Vinho in Porto, Madeira...
- Historic yearly fairs: the Golegã horse fair, St Matthew's Fair in Viseu, the March Fair in Aveiro, Feiras Novas in Ponte de Lima...
- Crafts fairs and the main Christmas markets and villages.

Each event has a short description, the place and a link to the official page. Fairs marked **(TBC)** have dates estimated from the previous edition. Those dates change when the organisers announce the official ones, usually one to three months ahead. Check the official page before you travel.

## How it works

The fairs are in [`feiras.json`](feiras.json). [`gerar_ics.py`](gerar_ics.py) checks the data and builds the English and Portuguese calendars, and GitHub Pages publishes them. Once a month an automated job looks for the official dates and new fairs; the rules it follows are in [`CLAUDE.md`](CLAUDE.md).
